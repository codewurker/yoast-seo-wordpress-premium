*** Yoast SEO premium Changelog ***


2026-08-18 - version 28.3
* add -  Improves the performance of the SEO optimization, by warming post and term cache when building data for the prominent words of the website.
* fix -  Fixes a bug where the editor would not scroll to the changes proposed by Yoast AI Optimize in WordPress 6.9 and higher.
* fix -  Fixes a bug where users without permission to manage redirects could delete existing redirects.
* update -  Bumps the minimum required version of Yoast SEO to 28.3.
* update -  Sets the minimum supported WordPress version to 6.9.
